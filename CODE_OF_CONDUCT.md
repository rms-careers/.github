# Code of Conduct

This organisation is a learning space for students, tutors and the RMS Careers team. We expect everyone who takes
part, in issues, pull requests and discussions, to:

- be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to programming or to GitHub;
- give feedback on code, not on the person, and explain the reason behind a suggestion;
- keep discussion on topic and in clear, professional language;
- never share another person's personal data, credentials or private messages;
- never post solutions to assignments or assessments.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and sharing others' private information are not accepted.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, tell your tutor or program coordinator, or write to
**engineering@rms-careers.com**. Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may remove content or block accounts that break this
code.
